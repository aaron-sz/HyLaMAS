how to read this
  CUT%    judger hit --max_new_tokens. Anything above ~2% means the Acc and
          Token columns are both partly measuring the cap. Raise the cap and
          re-run before believing either number.
  NOBOX%  judger never emitted \boxed{}. Those samples were graded on the last
          number appearing anywhere in the text -- often a figure from its own
          scratch work. High here with low Acc is an extraction failure, not a
          reasoning failure.
  PLCHLD% judger emitted a oxed{} whose contents have no digits -- almost
          always because it quoted the prompt's own oxed{YOUR_FINAL_ANSWER}
          late in a ramble. The grader takes the LAST box, so these are graded
          on the placeholder even when the right number appears earlier.
  tok|ok  mean output tokens over samples that finished. Compare against a
          `--method baseline` run on the SAME model: if latent_mas is not
          well below it, the judger is re-deriving rather than inheriting,
          i.e. it is ignoring the shared workspace.
  cache   KV positions in the workspace when the judger started.
